SideTracker Win98 font

The proportional bitmap text in Win98.ptfont derives from the FontStruction
“MS Sans Serif” and “MS Sans Serif Bold” by lou, distributed with 98.css.
Both original fonts and this bitmap adaptation are licensed under Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported.

Regular source: https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384746
Bold source: https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384862
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode
98.css source: https://github.com/jdan/98.css

The glyphs were rasterized with 4-bit antialiasing at five Tracker UI
sizes. The small regular styles use slightly larger, heavier glyphs.
Missing ASCII symbols and eight transport glyph cells were drawn for
the Tracker interface. FontStruct is copyright ©2017 Rob Meek.
