# Perf FX

- **Live** — Performance has 12 effects with four presets each. Hold a pad to apply its preset. The latest held pad in a column wins. Release the last pad to fade out, or press Delete to release all live pads. The eight buttons below the screen toggle track mutes. Press Perform to switch banks.
- **Patterns** — Pick **X — Perf FX** in FX1 or FX2, including on steps without notes. Use the Performance pad layout to select a preset. Use the jog wheel or arrows for release commands. The grid uses uppercase codes and the title bar shows the full setting.
- **Playback** — Up to three live or sequenced effects sound together. A fourth waits for a free lane. Muted tracks and steps that fail Chance skip their commands. Effects stay active through blank steps and same-pattern loops. Switching patterns or restarting playback releases them.
- **Line In and Mix** — In **Master → Global Mixer 3/3**, unmute and raise **Line In** to run it through the rack. Hold **F5** and turn the jog wheel or use the arrows to adjust **Perf FX Mix**. Delete while holding F5 resets it to 100%.
- **Recording and export** — The master WAV includes sequenced Perf FX in pattern, selection, song, and stems exports. Individual stems and direct Line In recordings are captured before the rack. Offline export mutes Line In.

| FX1 | FX2 | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `XLP3` | `XDE2` | Start Low Pass at 800 Hz and Dub Echo at 1/8 dotted |
| `----` | `----` | Keep both active |
| `XLP-` | `----` | Release Low Pass |
| `XOFF` | `----` | Release all sequenced effects |
