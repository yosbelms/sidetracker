Performance FX

Read guide
Stock Tracker+
Perform pads change assignable track and instrument settings, including synth macros.
SideTracker
Hold a pad to trigger a preset from the 12-effect master rack. Each effect has four presets. Up to three effects stack across the mix, including monitored Line In. The eight soft keys toggle track mutes, and Master has a dedicated wet/dry control.

Performance FX sequencing

Read guide
Stock Tracker+
FX1 and FX2 sequence built-in step commands such as instrument modulation and synth macros.
SideTracker
X — Perf FX can start or release a master effect from either FX slot, even on a step without a note. Effects hold across blank steps. Master exports include this automation.

Sample Editor

Read guide
Stock Tracker+
Choose a built-in sample effect and apply edits one at a time.
SideTracker
Build chains of up to eight stages, audition the chain in realtime when it fits the audio budget, and save it as a reusable patch. Four ST effects offer 24 types for saturation, modulation, diffusion, and texture.

Synth engines

Read guide
Stock Tracker+
Polyend’s factory synth engines and patch editor.
SideTracker
Adds MINI, a three-oscillator ladder monosynth, and OBER, a polysynth with PWM, hard sync, and SEM filter morphing. The Community Edition’s CHIP engine is also available.
